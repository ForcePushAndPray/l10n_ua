Ukraine HR Attendance Sheet

Табель обліку робочого часу (форма П-5)

Features

  • Monthly Timesheet - Табель обліку робочого часу
  • Ukrainian Codes - Standard codes (Я, В, Х, ВД, etc.)
  • Automatic Generation - Auto-fill based on schedule
  • Holiday Integration - Sync with vacations and sick leaves
  • Production Calendar - Ukrainian holidays support
  • Night Hours - Night shift tracking
  • Overtime - Overtime hours tracking
  • Report П-5 - Official form printing

Timesheet Codes

  • Я - Working day
  • В - Weekend/Holiday
  • Х - Sick leave
  • ВД - Business trip
  • ВП - Vacation
  • НН - Absence without reason

Requires l10n_ua_hr_base and l10n_ua_hr_holidays modules
